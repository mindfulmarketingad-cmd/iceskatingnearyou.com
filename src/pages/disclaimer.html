Accuracy of hours, prices, and sessions

Our listings come from public business listing data, imported in bulk and refreshed periodically. We work to keep it accurate, but we cannot guarantee that any detail is complete or current.

Rink schedules are especially changeable. Public skate times shift around hockey games, figure skating sessions, tournaments, private rentals, holidays, and maintenance. Outdoor and seasonal rinks depend on the weather.

Prices, skate rental availability, and age or skill requirements can also change without notice. Always confirm details directly with the rink before you travel.

Feature tags

Features such as indoor, outdoor, hockey, figure skating, and lessons are assigned automatically based on each business's own listing text. They may be incomplete or occasionally wrong. A missing tag does not mean a rink lacks that feature.

Safety

Ice skating is a physical activity with an inherent risk of falls and injury. Follow each rink's rules and posted safety guidance, wear appropriate gear, and skate within your ability. Supervise children closely.

Natural ice is never guaranteed safe. Ponds, lakes, and rivers can have thin or weak ice even in cold weather. Our listings do not assess ice conditions, so follow local authority guidance and posted warnings.
