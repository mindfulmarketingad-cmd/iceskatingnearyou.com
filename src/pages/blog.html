What we write about

We are not a lifestyle blog. Every guide answers a question skaters ask us before their first session, their kid's first lesson, or their first time on a frozen pond.

We stick to what holds true at almost any rink: technique, gear, fit, sharpening, safety and how rinks actually run. Anything that changes from rink to rink, like prices, session times and rental sizes, we tell you to check with the rink rather than guess.
